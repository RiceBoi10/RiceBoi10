About me

I'm entering my third-year of pharmacy in September 2026 at the University of Toronto. I'm building on a strong foundation in health sciences from my Bachelor of Science degree, where I developed a deep understanding of pharmacology, biochemistry, and patient-centered care.

My journey into pharmacy grew from a genuine belief that pharmacists are among the most accessible healthcare professionals. I've carried that belief through every role — from working in retail pharmacy to hospital settings, I've consistently prioritized patient education and medication safety.

Beyond the dispensary, I'm passionate about teaching and mentorship — currently supporting International Pharmacy Graduates as a student lab assistant with the faculty — and I'm committed to lifelong learning in this dynamic profession.
